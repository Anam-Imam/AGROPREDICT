PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: May 2026

Smart Agriculture ("the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses and uses information to provide its services.

Information Accessed

1. Internet Access
The App requires an internet connection to retrieve weather data, process predictions, and provide agricultural recommendations.

2. Photos and Images
The App allows users to select images from their device gallery for crop disease detection and analysis. Selected images are used solely to generate prediction results and improve the functionality of the App.

3. Location Information
The App may access the device's location to provide accurate, location-based weather information and forecasts relevant to the user's area.

How Information Is Used

The information accessed by the App is used exclusively for the following purposes:

• Providing weather forecasts and weather-related insights.
• Analyzing selected crop images for disease detection.
• Delivering agricultural recommendations and decision-support services.
• Maintaining and improving the performance and functionality of the App.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are implemented to protect information accessed by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The App may utilize third-party services and APIs to provide weather information, prediction processing, and related functionality. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms of use.

User Consent

By using the App, users consent to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in functionality, legal requirements, or service improvements. Any updates will be posted within the latest version of the policy.

Contact Information

For questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the App developer.
